HOW TO PLAY

Swipe a rider into a neighbor to swap. Line up 3 or more of the same rider and they burst into stardust.

Match 4 for a Comet that clears a line. Match an L or T for a Nova blast. Match 5 for a Stardust Orb: swap it with any rider to clear every one of them.

Hit the score and collect goal before your moves run out.
