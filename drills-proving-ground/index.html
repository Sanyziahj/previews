HOW TO DRILL

FIELD MANUAL

Move WASD or arrows. Left stick on a phone. Crouch with C. Still and low in the stealth kit veils you from cones.

Aim with the mouse, or drag the aim pad. Fire is left click. Scope is right click, Shift, or the SCOPE button. Inside the glass, distance and wind sit on the reticle.

Steady Breath (Space, or BREATH while scoped) slows the moment and settles the sway. Gadget is Q, or BREATH while you are not scoped: suppressor, decoy ping, heat vent, or a charged rail.

1–4 swaps the kit. Esc or PAUSE holds the range. Gamepad: sticks, RT fire, LT scope, RB breath, A crouch, B gadget, Start pause.

Pop-ups, spinning plates, patrol drones, and armored bots. Bullseyes and glowing cores throw a short stardust slow-mo. No mess, just drill.
